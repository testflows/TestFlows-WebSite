<section class="pricing-section">
<h2>Compute</h2>
<p>A session is charged by the hour for the units it reserves. A unit is 1 vCPU, 2G of memory and 16G of disk. A session that asks for more memory or disk than its vCPUs come with is charged for the larger number of units.</p>
<div class="pricing-table-wrap">
<table class="pricing-table">
<thead>
<tr><th>Class</th><th>Description</th><th>₡ an hour for each unit</th></tr>

<tr><td><code>standard-ie</code></td><td>Reduced performance</td><td>₡0.12</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>
<p>A class costs the same on every plan. <code>machine sessions classes</code> lists the classes and their current prices.</p>
</section>

<section class="pricing-section pricing-faq">
<h2>Questions</h2>

<details>
<summary>What is a credit?</summary>
<p>Credits pay for the vCPU-hours your sessions use. One vCPU for one hour costs ₡0.15 on <code>standard-ip</code> and ₡0.12 on <code>standard-ie</code>. Machine writes an amount of credits with the sign ₡, so ₡0.15 is 0.15 credits. A credit has no cash value.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How is a session charged?</summary>
<p>A running session is charged an hour ahead, for the units it reserves at its class's price. When it stops, the unused part of its last hour is given back. <code>machine account activity</code> lists every charge.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to credits I do not use?</summary>
<p>Plan credits renew at the start of each period, and the ones left over expire. Usage credits do not expire.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I buy usage credits?</summary>
<p>Usage credits are bought on top of any plan at €1 a credit, in packs of ₡5, ₡10, ₡25 and ₡50. Buy them on the account page or with <code>machine account buy-usage</code>.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which plan is right for me?</summary>
<p>Free shows what Machine does on your own programs. Starter fits one developer's everyday testing. Plus fits larger sessions and more of them. Pro fits long recordings, heavy test suites and heavy exploration. For machines reserved for your team, contact sales@testflows.com.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when I cancel?</summary>
<p>The plan stays until it renews, then the account moves to Free. Your usage credits stay.</p>
</details>
