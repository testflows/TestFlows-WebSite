## 1. General rules

Use the Marks only to refer to our products, truthfully, and in a way that
does not confuse anyone about who makes what. In particular:

- Do not use the Marks in a way that suggests we sponsor, endorse or are
  affiliated with you, your product or your service, unless we are.
- Do not use the Marks, or a variation, abbreviation, misspelling, phonetic
  equivalent or translation of them, in the name of your product, service,
  company, project, domain name, social media account or package.
- Do not call a modified version of our software by our Marks.

## 2. Names on registries and services

Only Katteli Inc. publishes or registers anything named after the Marks. No
one else may use a name that is `testflows`, starts with `testflows.`,
`testflows-` or `testflows_`, or otherwise contains the Marks, for anything
published or registered on any registry or service.

A fork of one of our repositories on a code hosting service may keep the
repository's name while it is used to work on our software or to contribute
to it. A fork published as a separate project follows Section 3.

## 3. Uses you may make

- **Referring to our products.** You may say that your software works with,
  runs on, or is tested with TestFlows Machine or the TestFlows Framework, if
  it is true. Use the word mark, not the logo.
- **Unmodified copies.** If you redistribute our open-source software
  unmodified, as its license allows, keep the Marks as they appear in it.
- **Modified copies and forks.** If you distribute a modified version of our
  open-source software, give it a name that does not contain the Marks, and
  remove the logo from it, including from its source. You may say truthfully
  that it is based on our software, for example “based on the TestFlows
  Framework”.
- **Services.** You may offer consulting, training or support for our
  products if you make clear that we do not provide them. “Support for
  TestFlows Machine” is allowed. “TestFlows Support” is not.
- **Writing and talks.** You may name our products in articles, books,
  courses, talks and reviews.
- **User groups.** A non-profit user group about our products may use the word
  mark in its name, such as “Berlin TestFlows Users”. It may charge only to
  cover the costs of its meetings. Ask us before running a conference that
  uses the Marks.

## 4. How to show the Marks

- Write TestFlows exactly, as one word with a capital T and F. Do not
  abbreviate, hyphenate, pluralize or combine it with other words.
- Use it as an adjective followed by the product, such as “TestFlows® Machine”
  or “the TestFlows® Framework”, not as a noun or a verb.
- Mark the first or most prominent use on a page or in a document with ®.
- Where you use the Marks, include this notice: “TestFlows is a registered
  trademark of Katteli Inc. in the United States.”

## 5. The logo

Use the logo only with our written permission. When you have it, use the logo
as we provide it. You may scale it. Do not change its colors, proportions or
shape, add to it, or combine it with another logo. A black and white version
is allowed where color cannot be used.
