## 2. Talking about our products

You may name our products to describe them truthfully. For example:

- “Our integration tests run on TestFlows Machine.”
- “This plugin works with the TestFlows Framework.”
- A blog post, book, course, talk or review about our products.

Use the name for this, not the logo. Nothing you say may suggest that we made,
sponsor or approve your product, service or event when we did not.

## 3. Copies, changes and forks of our open-source code

- **A copy left as we published it** keeps our Marks exactly where they
  appear in it.
- **A changed version** is your own project. Give it a name of its own that
  does not contain TestFlows, and take our logo out of it, including from its
  source files. You may describe where it came from, for example “derived
  from the TestFlows Framework”.
- **A fork made to contribute** to one of our repositories may keep the
  repository’s name while it serves that purpose.

## 4. Names reserved for us

Only Katteli Inc. names things TestFlows. No one else may use TestFlows, or a
name that resembles it, sounds like it, translates it or shortens it, as the
name or part of the name of:

- a product, service, project, company or business;
- a package, library, container image, plugin or extension, on any registry,
  store or marketplace, including anything named `testflows`, `testflows.*`,
  `testflows-*` or `testflows_*`;
- an organization, account, channel or handle on any service;
- a domain name.

Two exceptions apply: a fork made to contribute, under Section 3, and a
community group, under Section 5.

## 5. Services and communities

- **Paid services.** You may sell consulting, training or support for our
  products if your customers can tell that the service is yours and not ours.
  Describe it with the product’s name, for example “training on TestFlows
  Machine”. Do not name the service itself after us, as in “TestFlows
  Training”.
- **Community groups.** A local or online group of people who use our
  products may carry the name, for example “TestFlows Meetup Toronto”, and
  use it for the group’s own pages, accounts and channels. The group must be
  run for its members and not for profit, and any fee may only cover what a
  meeting costs to hold. A domain name for the group, and any conference or
  larger event that uses our Marks, need our written agreement first.

## 6. Writing the name

- In text, write **TestFlows**: one word, with a capital T and a capital F.
  Do not shorten it, split it, change its case or make it plural.
- Follow it with what it names: “TestFlows Machine”, “the TestFlows
  Framework”. Do not use TestFlows alone as a noun or a verb.
- When you write about our products, add ® to the first or most visible
  mention on each page or in each document, and include this notice:
  “TestFlows is a registered trademark of Katteli Inc. in the United States.”

Our logo is used only where we have agreed to it in writing, and only in the
files we provide. It may be resized, and shown in a single color where color
is not available. It may not otherwise be redrawn, recolored, cropped,
animated or placed inside another logo.
