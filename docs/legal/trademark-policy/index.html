Trademark Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

TestFlows® is a registered trademark of Katteli Inc. in the United States. The TestFlows logo is also a trademark of Katteli Inc. In this policy, the name and the logo are “our Marks”.

A developer who sees the name TestFlows should be able to trust that the software or service behind it comes from us. This policy says how others can talk about our products, build on them and run communities around them, without weakening that trust. It applies wherever our Marks are used, whether or not a particular use is covered by a registration.

1. Open source and our Marks

The TestFlows Framework, the TestFlows Machine SDKs and the TestFlows Machine examples are published under the Apache License 2.0. That license lets you use, change and share the code. It gives no rights in our Marks, as its Section 6 states. This policy is what governs the name and the logo, including in copies of that code.

2. Talking about our products

You may name our products to describe them truthfully. For example:

  • “Our integration tests run on TestFlows Machine.”
  • “This plugin works with the TestFlows Framework.”
  • A blog post, book, course, talk or review about TestFlows.

Use the name for this, not the logo. Nothing you say may suggest that we made, sponsor or approve your product, service or event when we did not.

3. Copies, changes and forks of our open-source code

  • A copy left as we published it keeps our Marks exactly where they appear in it.
  • A changed version is your own project. Give it a name of its own that does not contain TestFlows, and take our logo out of it, including from its source files. You may describe where it came from, for example “derived from the TestFlows Framework”.
  • A fork made to contribute to one of our repositories may keep the repository’s name while it serves that purpose.

4. Names reserved for us

Only Katteli Inc. names things TestFlows. No one else may use TestFlows, or a name that resembles it, sounds like it, translates it or shortens it, as the name or part of the name of:

  • a product, service, project, company or business;
  • a package, library, container image, plugin or extension, on any registry, store or marketplace, including anything named testflows, testflows.*, testflows-* or testflows_*;
  • an organization, account, channel or handle on any service;
  • a domain name.

Section 5 makes one exception, for community groups.

5. Services and communities

  • Paid services. You may sell consulting, training or support for our products if your customers can tell that the service is yours and not ours. Describe it with the product’s name, for example “training on TestFlows Machine”. Do not name the service itself after us, as in “TestFlows Training”.
  • Community groups. A local or online group of people who use our products may carry the name, for example “TestFlows Meetup Toronto”, and use it for the group’s own pages, accounts and channels. The group must be run for its members and not for profit, and any fee may only cover what a meeting costs to hold. A domain name for the group, and any conference or larger event that uses our Marks, need our written agreement first.

6. Writing the name

  • In text, write TestFlows: one word, with a capital T and a capital F. Do not shorten it, split it, change its case or make it plural.
  • Follow it with what it names: “TestFlows Machine”, “the TestFlows Framework”. Do not use TestFlows alone as a noun or a verb.
  • When you write about our products, add ® to the first or most visible mention on each page or in each document, and include this notice: “TestFlows is a registered trademark of Katteli Inc. in the United States.”

7. Our logo

Our logo is used only where we have agreed to it in writing, and only in the files we provide. It may be resized, and shown in a single colour where colour is not available. It may not otherwise be redrawn, recoloured, cropped, animated or placed inside another logo.

8. Contact

To ask about a use this policy does not cover, to request our agreement, or to tell us about a misuse of our Marks, write to legal@testflows.com.
