Service use. Credits pay only for eligible Services at the prices then in effect. Included Credits are deducted before Usage Credits.

Final purchases. Credit purchases are final unless a refund is required by law or authorized by Katteli or its merchant of record. Partial refunds of Usage Credit packs are not available. Duration. Usage Credits remain valid while the account is open. If you close your account, unused Usage Credits are retained for 90 days — reactivate within that window to keep them; after 90 days they are forfeited. Included Credits end on the date communicated when they are granted (typically the end of the plan billing period) and do not roll over unless we expressly say so; closing an account does not extend them beyond that date.

Service allowance only. A Credit is only a right to consume eligible Services. It cannot be exchanged for cash and is not currency.

Account restricted. Credits cannot be resold, assigned, moved to another account, or used by anyone other than the account holder.

Automatic purchase. If we offer automatic purchases, enabling the feature authorizes the merchant of record to charge your selected payment method, including applicable taxes, whenever the chosen balance threshold is reached, until you disable the feature.

Merchant of record. Purchases through Stripe Checkout with Managed Payments are charged by Stripe (via Link) as merchant of record. See the Terms of Service and Stripe’s terms and privacy policy for the payment transaction.

Subscription Included Credits and resource entitlements apply only during the paid period for which they were granted. Upgrades, downgrades, and cancellations follow the Terms of Service and the billing portal flows we provide. A plan change does not convert Credits into cash.
